About

I'm a mechanical engineer working on one-way attack UAS at Empirical Systems Aerospace. My scope runs from propulsion and fuel management to control surfaces, wing deployment mechanisms, and composite structures — I like problems where the constraints are tight and the part still has to fly.

Before this I designed pump and piping systems at Wallace Group Engineering, and before that I was doing whatever paid the bills: ocean lifeguard, speargun manufacturing technician, rock climbing gym employee. Each one taught me something about working under pressure and getting things right the first time.

I grew up in San Clemente, surfing and diving, which is probably why I gravitate toward building things that have to perform in the real world and not just on a screen. Cal Poly's learn-by-doing approach suited me — I put in over 200 hours in the machine shops on projects that had nothing to do with a grade.

I care about design for manufacturability. A part that's brilliant in CAD and impossible to build isn't a good design.
